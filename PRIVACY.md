## English

### Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any user data whatsoever.**

This extension:

- ✅ Works entirely locally in your browser
- ✅ Does not collect personal information
- ✅ Does not track browsing history
- ✅ Does not store cookies or user preferences
- ✅ Does not transmit data to external servers
- ✅ Does not use analytics or tracking services

### What the Extension Does

The extension serves a single purpose: automatically adding Monzonline affiliate tags to Amazon URLs when you visit supported Amazon websites.

### Permissions Used

- **webNavigation**: Required to detect when you navigate to Amazon pages
- **tabs**: Required to redirect to modified URLs with affiliate tags
- **Host permissions**: Limited to Amazon domains only (amazon.de)

### Data Processing

All URL modifications happen locally in your browser. No data leaves your device.

### Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties because we don't collect any data.

### Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact the extension developer through the Chrome Web Store.
